## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a refined Islamic literary atmosphere. It bridges classical scholarship with contemporary digital ergonomics, evoking the serene focus of traditional Islamic manuscript reading rooms translated into a fluid, highly accessible modern interface.

### Brand Personality & Target Audience
- **Scholarly & Reverent:** Grounded in intellectual heritage, treating academic texts, sacred volumes, and classical literature with visual gravity.
- **Warm & Hospitable:** Clean, airy, and welcoming rather than cold or overly bureaucratic.
- **Editorial Precision:** Balanced proportions, disciplined baseline grids, generous reading line heights, and delicate framing lines.
- **Target Audience:** Scholars, university students, avid readers, researchers, and patrons across the Arab world seeking curated physical and digital texts.

### Design Movement
The visual language merges **Modern Editorial Typography** with **Restrained Classical Ornamentation**. Surface hierarchy favors tactile paper qualities—creamy ivory backdrops, subtle organic borders, and deliberate typographic rhythm—punctuated by warm gold indicators and deep forest green authority anchors. Restrained geometric motifs appear strictly at faint opacities (4–6%) as watermark rules, section dividers, or subtle header backgrounds.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in classical Islamic bookbinding traditions, harmonizing forest greens with warm ivory parchment and burnished gold leaf.

### Primary Palette
- **Primary Brand Green (`#0F4C3A`):** The primary anchor for top-tier actions, prominent navigation headers, and focused states.
- **Dark Accent Green (`#083328`):** Deep forest green reserved for deep contrast containers, footers, and prominent text anchors.
- **Primary Hover (`#0B3F31`):** Interaction state for primary green interactive elements.

### Secondary & Accent Palette
- **Warm Gold Accent (`#A47925`):** Restrained gold applied to key editorial rules, decorative badges, active indicator tabs, and scholastic highlights.
- **Gold Highlight (`#B58B3A`):** Accent hover states and subtle rating indicators.
- **Gold Tint Surface (`#F5EDD9`):** Low-contrast gold wash for featured badge fills and selected card containers.

### Surfaces & Neutrals
- **Canvas / Background (`#FCFBF8`):** Warm ivory foundation providing eye comfort during prolonged reading sessions.
- **Surface Subtle (`#F6F4EE`):** Slightly recessed ivory tone for secondary sections, sidebars, and grouped content tiers.
- **Surface Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity elevated cards, search fields, text inputs, and modals.
- **Border Default (`#D7DED9`):** Soft botanical-grey border defining cards and dividers without visual friction.
- **Border Strong (`#AEBBB4`):** High-definition dividers, active input borders, and outline chips.

### Typography Colors
- **Text Primary (`#1F2A26`):** Deep charcoal with a hint of warm green, avoiding the harshness of pure black.
- **Text Secondary (`#53615B`):** Muted green-charcoal for subtitles, authors, metadata, and supporting narrative.
- **Text Muted (`#718078`):** Subtle captions, placeholder text, and timestamps.

### Semantic Status
- **Success:** `#176B4D` (Background: `#E5F3EC`)
- **Warning / Pending:** `#8A5A00` (Background: `#FFF4D6`)
- **Error:** `#B42318` (Background: `#FDE9E7`)

## Typography

The typographic system is built on a bilingual foundation with first-class right-to-left (`dir="rtl"`) execution. 

### Font Roles
- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** `Noto Serif` provides classical presence, evoking historical Arabic and Latin press traditions. In RTL contexts, headline pairings fall back gracefully to classical Arabic serif weights (`Amiri` or `Cairo` where configured).
- **Body & Functional UI:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides crisp contemporary clarity with high legibility across catalog listings, specs, and patron account interfaces. In Arabic layout contexts, it harmonizes with `IBM Plex Sans Arabic`.

### Typographic Rules
- **Line Heights:** Body copy line height remains at `1.65` or higher to ensure Arabic diacritics (tashkeel) and descending glyphs breathe naturally without clipping.
- **Directionality:** All layout text defaults to RTL, with directional layout flipping (start/end alignment) strictly respected across typography helpers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous page margins, reflecting classic book margins and physical manuscript folios.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-sm` (1rem / 16px), `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px). Single-column book feeds and full-width card stacks.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin` (1.5rem / 24px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). 2-column or 3-column book catalog grid.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, max-width `1280px` centered, `margin-lg` (3rem / 48px), `gutter-lg` (2rem / 32px). 4-column book grids with fixed sidebar filters.

### Vertical Rhythm
Spacings rely on a consistent 4px/8px incremental rhythm. Section boundaries utilize `space-xl` (32px) or larger multipliers (`48px` / `64px`) to preserve an unhurried, editorial reading cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through warm tonal layering and soft paper shadows, avoiding stark artificial drops.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Parchment Foundation):** Background canvas (`#FCFBF8`) and grouped container surfaces (`#F6F4EE`) with zero shadow. Separation is achieved through subtle `1px solid #D7DED9` outlines.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** White paper surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) sitting slightly above the warm canvas. Employs a fine outline (`1px solid #D7DED9`) paired with an ambient tinted shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(24, 42, 34, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(24, 42, 34, 0.04);`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover):** Applied to active book cards on hover, popovers, and sticky navigation:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(24, 42, 34, 0.08), 0 2px 4px rgba(24, 42, 34, 0.04);`
  Border transitions to `Border Strong` (`#AEBBB4`).
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Deep dialogs, patron cart trays, and reading view drawers:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgba(8, 51, 40, 0.14), 0 4px 8px rgba(8, 51, 40, 0.06);`

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape rules, balancing traditional bound-volume sharpness with soft, ergonomic touch targets.

### Radius Scale
- **Small (`rounded` / 6px to 8px):** Form controls, text input boxes, dropdown selectors, and compact buttons.
- **Medium (`rounded-lg` / 12px to 16px):** Catalog cards, manuscript preview containers, filter sidebars, and dialogue modals.
- **Fully Rounded (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Classification pills, status tags, author avatars, and quick-filter chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0F4C3A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `6px`. On hover, background shifts to `#0B3F31`. Active scale `0.98`. Focus ring: 2px offset with `#A47925`.
- **Secondary / Scholastic:** Background `#F5EDD9`, text `#0F4C3A`, border `1px solid #A47925`. On hover, background `#EFE2C2`.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#1F2A26`, border `1px solid #D7DED9`. On hover, surface changes to `#F6F4EE` with `#0F4C3A` text.
- **Directional Notes:** In RTL mode, leading icons appear on the right side with `8px` spacing.

### Book Cover Display Card
- **Aspect Ratio:** Strictly locked to uncropped `2:3` proportion representing physical book editions.
- **Frame:** Housed in an elevated card (`#FFFFFF`) with `1px solid #D7DED9` border and `8px` to `12px` roundedness.
- **Spine Effect:** A subtle `inset 2px 0 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)` (or right inset in RTL) applied to book cover thumbnails to mirror a physical binding groove.
- **Metadata Tier:** Book title rendered in `headline-sm` (`Noto Serif`), author in `body-sm` (`Text Secondary`), category tag in a pill badge.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Pills:** Background `#F6F4EE`, text `#53615B`, border `1px solid #D7DED9`, border-radius `9999px`, padding `4px 12px`.
- **Gold Accent Badges:** Background `#F5EDD9`, text `#A47925`, border `1px solid #B58B3A` for special manuscripts, rare prints, and staff picks.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Structure:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D7DED9`, border-radius `6px`, padding `10px 14px`, text `#1F2A26`.
- **States:** Focus produces border `#0F4C3A` with a soft box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(15, 76, 58, 0.12)`.
- **Search Header:** Includes quick clear action and search icon aligned to the inline-start position.

### Lists & Index Items
- **Editorial Dividers:** Border bottom `1px solid #D7DED9`. Optional decorative divider using a centered, 4% opacity eight-pointed star ornament.
- **Interactive Rows:** Background `#FFFFFF` transitioning to `#F6F4EE` on hover with a right-aligned indicator accent bar in `#0F4C3A`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** 18px square with 4px border radius. Unchecked border `1.5px solid #AEBBB4`. Checked state filled with `#0F4C3A` and white check icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** 18px circle with an inner `#0F4C3A` dot of 8px when selected.

### Islamic Geometric Accents
- Low-opacity (4% to 6%) vector linear geometric motifs utilized as container watermark backdrops or decorative card headers, executed in `#0F4C3A` or `#A47925`.
